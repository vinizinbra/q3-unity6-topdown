# Street Punk style test v1

Single idle design study generated with the built-in image generation tool. Uses the user's three attached original world roster images as the style sources rather than generated Neon sheets. Not an approved production replacement or imported gameplay asset.

Image: `Assets/0_Refs/NeonPunks/StreetPunk-StyleTest-v1.png`.

## Prompt

Create ONE SINGLE full-body idle sprite of a normal NEON STREET PUNK enemy, on a genuine transparent background. This is an art-style matching test for an EXISTING GAME, not a fresh illustration style. The THREE most recently attached user images (Frost Wights/Arctic Security/Frozen Wildlife, Rukks/Security/Wildlife, Black Moles/Security/Wildlife) are the ONLY visual style sources. The sprite must look hand-drawn by precisely the SAME ARTIST as those three images and belong naturally beside the Rukk Grunt and Mole Grunt. Study their proportions and rendering: compact low silhouette, enormous head directly nestled into shoulders with almost no neck, very short mostly concealed legs, simple solid trapezoid feet, torso a compact wedge tilted forward, one visible broad hand with only minimal finger division, weapons and equipment rendered as simple broad planar masses. Outer outline firm clean black but not inflated cartoon marker thickness. Sparse thin internal separations. Hard polygonal cel shading with 2-3 related values and a few irregular broad angular shade planes exactly like the reference orange Rukk skin, brown Mole fur and cream robot shells. Not uniformly flat icon art, not soft glossy cartoon shading. Their faces are restrained aggressive graphic masks: tiny angular eyes, no large expressive human eyes, no enormous smiling mouth or toothy grin. Apply that same restraint to a HUMAN punk without turning him into an animal. Design: small wiry street thug, warm muted tan skin, angular head with a severe wedge brow, small narrow red eyes, straight small closed mouth, one jagged dark-purple swept hair mass with ONE magenta colored crest plane rather than a huge elaborate fan mohawk. Black sleeveless cropped jacket, one simple purple neck scarf analogous to the Rukk/Mole scarf, dark short trousers largely hidden by torso, two very short chunky black boots. ONE oversized dark metal knuckleduster glove on the forward fist, angular rectangular planes and ONE flat magenta painted stripe; the other hand small and bare, held slightly behind. Tiny simple dark wrist cuff. NO crystals or gemstones, no magical cracks, no spikes of corruption, no guitar, no speakers, no cybernetic armor suit, no giant shoulder pads, no glowing aura, no sunglasses. Whole character angled toward screen-right in the exact same slightly elevated three-quarter viewing angle as Rukk Grunt/Mole Grunt; do not exaggerate overhead view. Pose crouched ready to advance, forward fist low and forward, spare hand tucked back, head tilted toward target. Feet grounded at the same implied baseline, natural small transparent negative gaps, no long athletic legs. Reference enemy-like proportions NOT a muscular chibi superhero, NOT an exaggerated big-grin mascot. Charcoal black clothing, muted purple scarf, tan skin, small magenta hair/equipment accents. Keep neon accents restrained so materials and facial design are doing the work. One full-body character centered with ample transparent padding, no duplicate views, no text or nameplates, no floor or shadow, no environment, no VFX, no particles, no background haze. Actual zero-alpha transparent background and opaque clean sprite interiors. The goal is unmistakable SAME-ARTIST continuity with the THREE USER REFERENCES, not resemblance to any previously generated Neon roster.
