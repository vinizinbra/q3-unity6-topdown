## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Redesign FOUR Neon City police SECURITY ROBOT regular enemies as one transparent source character sheet, ONE IDLE POSE of each only. The TWO provided ORIGINAL GAME ROSTERS are the direct art-style anchors. Match the SAME ARTIST as their security bots: compact squat machine bodies, broad wedge feet, oversized functional equipment, no realistic human anatomy, clean confident black outline, sparse interior lines, cream armor with 2-3 broad angular off-white shading planes, graphite mechanical joints and simple flat LED details. NO crystals or gemstones. Do not copy animals or fantasy characters. The previous Neon robots felt generic: these must have four unmistakably DIFFERENT engineering silhouettes, clear police identity and purposeful forms, same product family through cream shell, charcoal recessed chassis, one small red-blue emergency beacon and cyan electronic accents. No realistic 3D gloss or soft gradients, no greebles, no decoration clutter, no exaggerated rounded toy proportions. All at same elevated three-quarter view facing right, top surfaces visible. Use a large landscape sheet with four spacious areas arranged 2x2 and transparent gutters, NO captions, NO names, NO grid, NO faction banners. ONE SHARED physical scale across robots: Patrol1.0 height, Riot1.25 height and1.5 bulk, Shielder0.65 height, Pursuit Drone0.45 height with modest wingspan, don't enlarge flyers to fill areas. Top-left PATROL BOT: LOW WIDE biped enforcement unit with HEAD MERGED INTO upper torso like a compact armored police helmet, a narrow RED visor recessed in a black horizontal face band, one large chunky rectangular cyan-muzzled blaster forming its right forearm, opposite small claw hand, two short sturdy wedge feet, asymmetrical armor, a simple black-white angular checker panel on one shoulder. NO separate cube head sitting on a skinny neck. Top-right RIOT BOT: heavy low forward-leaning block-shaped biped with helmet tucked behind ONE HUGE cream rectangular riot shield, shield is main silhouette on screen-right, charcoal border and a single bold cyan vertical viewing slit, small recessed red sensor face visible above shield edge, chunky exposed opposite fist holding a SHORT dark stun baton, no long legs, machine looks like a walking barricade, no human-like shoulder/waist anatomy. Bottom-left SHIELDER BOT: hovering SUPPORT UNIT, unique inverted-U or horseshoe-shaped cream chassis surrounding ONE suspended circular cyan projector, small dark sensor pod at top, two short blocky downward emitter prongs forming U tips, small red-blue beacon on top. NO fins like World1 security drone, NO rotors, NO handheld shield, NO gun, NO arms/legs; distinguish by U-shaped silhouette and central projector. Bottom-right PURSUIT DRONE: small aerodynamic police flyer, angular flat wedge central body with SINGLE red camera eye at pointed front, TWO compact charcoal ducted fans embedded in cream stubby wings tilted toward viewer, short downward camera/gun module, one tiny red-blue police beacon behind camera, no legs. Clear aircraft silhouette unlike floating shielder. Both flyers much smaller physically than grounded bots, leave ample empty transparent space. Keep the original references' sprite drawing language EXACTLY. All four designs complete and fully visible inside generous margins, weapons uncut, no overlap. Opaque flat-colored sprite interiors with clean anti-aliased edges, ACTUAL transparent alpha background, NO tinted haze, NO shadow, NO ground, NO floor, NO environment, NO shield bubble or aura, NO projectiles, NO VFX, NO text or watermark. These are design proposals for four regular enemies, not bosses or elites. Balanced visual detail like original roster bots, render broad geometric highlights rather than noisy comic detail.
