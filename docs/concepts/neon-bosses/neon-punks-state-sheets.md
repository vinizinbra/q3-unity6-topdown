## Light sheet prompt

Reference1 is approved enemy identities and simple angular style; reference2 establishes intended gameplay relative sizes. Preserve the specific approved punk designs, warm tan skin, bold black contour, geometric flat 2-3 tone cel shading, large graphic angular masses, simplified hands and wedge boots, NO texture/gradients/soft rendering. No crystals or gemstones on any normal enemy. No animals, no robots, no heavy punks, no boss. Large landscape canvas, uniform 3 COLUMN x 3 ROW frame layout with generous transparent margins and full body/equipment inside every frame. Row1 ONLY STREET PUNK in three states: col1 IDLE compact crouched stance, short pink mohawk, purple neck scarf and vest, single massive plain dark punching gauntlet; col2 WINDUP pulls that same gauntlet back with torso twist, free hand forward; col3 ATTACK extends that same gauntlet forward in a clear powerful punch, feet planted. Row2 ONLY AMP GUNNER: purple swept spiky hair, opaque magenta goggles, purple black clothing, ONE dark backpack speaker with magenta cone and short cable, chunky dark sonic gun with cyan muzzle. col1 IDLE carrying gun lowered slightly; col2 AIM WINDUP braces gun and aims forward with clear change of pose; col3 FIRE leans back with gun recoil, no muzzle flash and no projectiles. Row3 ONLY RAIL SKATER: smooth black protective helmet with tiny magenta crest, cyan eye visor, purple sleeveless clothing, dark elbow pads, chunky inline roller skates with magenta wheels, short pink energy baton. col1 IDLE low skating stance; col2 WINDUP crouches lower drawing baton back; col3 DASH ATTACK body leans strongly forward, one leg pushes back and baton thrusts forward, both skates fully visible, no motion trails. Every row depicts exactly the SAME enemy with consistent clothing, weapon handedness and features across poses. Same slightly elevated three-quarter view facing screen-right, matching reference's top-down-friendly sprites. A SHARED WORLD SCALE: normal Street Punk head-to-sole height approximately 200 pixels on a 1536x1024 output, Amp Gunner same body scale with speaker reaching about 220 pixels, crouching Rail Skater around 170 pixels high. Do NOT enlarge any pose to fill a frame; action can extend wider but body scale stays fixed. One consistent ground baseline per row, clear empty space between sprites, at least 40px padding around every silhouette. Background must be ACTUAL TRANSPARENT ALPHA, zero alpha outside sprite silhouettes, no colored fog, no painted backdrop, no shadows. No labels, names, headings, grid lines, badges, environment, rings, VFX, projectiles, particles, floor, glow aura, crystals or extra characters. Clean cutout asset sprites with fully opaque interiors and sharp antialiased edges. Maintain simple chunky graphic art rather than detailed comic illustration. These are the three light enemies, not miniature versions of Riff Riot: no guitar, no giant shoulder amplifiers, no excessive muscle.
