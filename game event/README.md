These are **RGB printer-review PDFs**, not certified PDF/X or CMYK press masters. Text is embedded; a simple typographic wordmark substitutes for the original custom logo, which was not located as a standalone master. Generated artwork matches the visual direction but is a new illustration, not the exact supplied character pose. This kit develops the mockup concept rather than reproducing every object in its scene.

Paper/board/card/sticker PDFs include 3 mm bleed on each side, with PDF TrimBox and BleedBox; page size is therefore 6 mm larger than trim in each dimension. White margins are intentional. No crop marks are added. Print at **100% / actual size**, not fit-to-page. Ask the printer to use the TrimBox. For home proofs on A4, the bleed makes the A4 PDF larger than the sheet: use A3 and trim, or scale a proof only (not production).

The 1102 × 1427 transparent character source gives approximately 313 ppi on the 70 × 90 mm sticker artwork placement, 313 ppi on the A4 sign, 101 ppi on the standee panel and 81 ppi on the backboard. Text and shards remain vector sharp. Approve a physical crop proof for large graphics; replace the illustration with a higher-resolution master for close viewing. Upscaling alone does not restore missing detail. The large board and standee are **review layouts**, not approved large-format artwork.

Neon pink and cyan are RGB design colors; normal CMYK printing will look less luminous. Have the printer convert using their material/profile and approve a proof. Do not assume a screen match or fluorescent inks. Obtain vendor templates for fabric and spot-color cutting paths; no machine-ready contour paths are included.

## Practical first order

Start with 1 A4 play sign, 1 A5 sticker sign, 50–100 cards and 50–100 stickers. Get a quote for one backboard and one standee, with supporting feet/easel included. Add the runner once table measurements are known. Quantities are suggestions, not vendor minimums or price estimates.
