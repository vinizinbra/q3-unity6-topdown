How a run works
~15 MIN
1
SURVIVE
3 MIN

Enemies never stop coming. Defeated ones drop XP and Coins.

Every level up, the game pauses and you pick 1 of 3 cards: upgrades for your Skill, your stats or a new weapon.

Low on health? Find a Healing Shrine.
2
BREAK
1 MIN

Every so often the waves stop. Clear the enemies left until AREA SECURED shows up. Then it's time to:

  • Buy weapons at the Store
  • Add weapon Perks at the Blacksmith
  • Risk a Cursed Rift for rewards
3
EXPLORE
★ More rewards

The map hides secrets. Go off the beaten path and hunt them down!

Challenges

Races against the clock or an extra round of combat. Find one and start it.

Rift Mutations

Clear a Challenge to earn a rare reward that changes your whole run.

Chests

Scattered across the map. Open the Map (M / R2) to find them all.
